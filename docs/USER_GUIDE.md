# Performance Tracker user guide

Performance Tracker is installed for one organization. Use only the pilot login ID issued by the Administrator and the password known only to you. The pilot uses dummy unique IDs; only name, rank or grade, organizational placement, and appointment may reflect the real organization.

## First sign-in

1. Enter the pilot login ID and temporary password.
2. Replace the temporary password when prompted. Do not return the new password to the Administrator.
3. If prompted, enroll the authenticator by adding the displayed secret to the authenticator application and entering the current six-digit code.
4. Check that the displayed name, appointment, and organization path are correct. Report errors to the Administrator before entering records.

Never share a password, authenticator code, recovery information, or browser session. Sign out on a shared device. Generic device notifications contain no performance details; open the application to read the actual notice.

## Appraisees

- Record activities and submit self-assessment entries for each applicable criterion.
- The assessment screen shows completed and total criteria and moves sequentially with Previous and Next.
- Ratings 1, 2, and 5 require a written justification and one valid single-page PDF or image, no larger than 5 MB. Evidence remains unavailable until malware scanning reports it clean.
- After Commander approval, acknowledge the appraisal in the application. The complaint deadline is exactly three calendar days from the system availability timestamp.
- A complaint stays open when overdue; deadlines count weekends and public holidays.

## Evaluators and supervisory leaders

- Work only on people shown in the snapshotted evaluator chain.
- Confirm activities and complete applicable ratings without changing organizational assignments.
- Restricted comments are for an accepted grievance only. Do not copy them into public comments, ordinary reports, screenshots, or tickets.
- The Commander provides final approval and authorized correction decisions but is excluded from personal evaluation in the pilot.

## Complaints and corrections

- Complaint submission: within three calendar days of appraisal availability.
- Acceptance or return: within three calendar days of complaint submission.
- Final decision: within five calendar days of acceptance.
- A correction authorization expires after five calendar days. Corrections create a new immutable version; the original appraisal remains viewable.

If the page reports an error, refresh once. If it persists, record the time, page, and non-sensitive error text. Do not put scores, complaint text, evidence, passwords, or authenticator codes in a support message.
