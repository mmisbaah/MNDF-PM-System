# December 2026 training plan

All December records are training data and must use dummy unique IDs and pilot login IDs. The System Authorizer enables training mode before exercises begin. Production mode is restored and verified before 1 January 2027.

## Training sequence

1. **Orientation — all 40 users:** sign-in, password replacement, MFA where required, navigation, notifications, sign-out, and safe support reporting.
2. **Personal records — appraisees and evaluators:** activities, sequential self-assessment, applicability, justification, evidence, evaluator-chain responsibilities, and reports.
3. **Governance — leaders and grievance officers:** approval, acknowledgement, complaints, restricted comments, deadlines, corrections, eligibility, and Authorizer handover.
4. **Operations — Administrator and Technical Operator:** personnel provisioning, seven-day approval, malware quarantine, scheduled jobs, monitoring alerts, encrypted backup, restoration, rollback, and incident response.

Each participant must complete the relevant practical scenario on the device they will use. The trainer records only pilot login ID, required module, completion status, date, and trainer—never passwords, authenticator secrets, appraisal text, or evidence.

## Completion criteria

- All 40 expected users complete orientation and their role modules.
- Every privileged user independently enrolls and verifies MFA.
- The attended scenarios in the operational readiness record have evidence references and status `PASS`.
- The 40-user capacity gate, December isolation gate, security regression, backup restoration, and full release gate pass.
- No severity-1 or severity-2 incident remains open.
